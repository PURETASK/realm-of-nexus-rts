# Five Domains – overview summary (recovered transcript)

> Source: an earlier assistant transcript summarising all five domains at a high level, pasted Sep 6, 2026. This is **not** a mechanical sheet. It gives themes, resources, key buildings and unit names only.
> Where it conflicts with the Volume 1 sheets (Abyss, Tempest), the Volume 1 sheets win.

| Domain | Core theme | Resource named | Key buildings | Key units | Visual style |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Radiance | Light, purity, celestial power | Sunstone | Solar Forge, Radiant Towers, Celestial Shrines | Sunforged Knights, Phoenix Heralds, Radiant Angels | Golden, shining architecture, beams of light |
| Verdance | Nature, growth, balance with the Everwood | Everwood Essence | Verdant Grove (resource), Everwood Barracks, Healing Garden | Verdant Sentinels, Spirits of the Everwood, Forest Nymphs | Lush forests, wood and stone, living vines |
| Abyss | Darkness, corruption, the Void | "Void Energy" (superseded by Soul Essence / Voidstone in Volume 1) | Void Summoning Chamber, Corrupted Barracks, Abyssal Forge (superseded) | Voidspawn, Corrupted Sentinels, Shadow Beasts (superseded) | Obsidian, glowing red cracks, shadow |
| Tempest | Storms, chaos, the Skyborn | Aether Crystals from storm clouds (matches Volume 1 secondary) | Lightning Spire, Aether Conduit, Thunder Forge (superseded) | Stormriders, Drakes, Wind Elementals | Floating structures, storm clouds, sharp metal |
| Sanctuary | Divine order, healing, celestial protection | Holy Light | Holy Shrine, Lightwell, Guardian Temple | Celestial Paladins, Seraphic Archers, Divine Griffins | Serene marble and gold, divine light |

Ability themes: Radiance heals and smites with sunlight; Verdance summons creatures, heals plants and grows forests and vines; Abyss corrupts, phases and weakens; Tempest controls weather, lightning and air; Sanctuary heals, banishes corruption and shields.

## How the game uses this

Radiance, Verdance and Sanctuary are implemented in `js/data/` as **provisional** factions built from this table plus the structure of the Abyss and Tempest sheets. Everything not in the table above (hero names, tier names, secondary resources, catalysts, unit stats, research) is invented and marked `provisional: true` in the data files. Replace it from the Volume 1 PDF when available.
