Findings — run 3, after the AI tech fix, Abyss drop-off and Sanctuary ward trim

  1. Sanctuary is strongest at 63 percent; Abyss is weakest at 28 percent. The rest sit between 28 and 63. A spread wider than about twenty points means the rosters, not the AI, are unbalanced.
  2. Tech. Tier 2 was reached in 286 of 300 seats; Tier 3 in 13. Tier 2 units built per faction across the run: Sanctuary 1, Tempest 13, Radiance 8, Verdance 39, Abyss 2.
  3. 25 matches stalled to the cap. Both sides turtle behind towers; the late-game attack escalation is meant to end these.
  4. Difficulty. The human seat won 17 of 50 on easy and 22 of 50 on hard. Only the income multiplier is asymmetric in this harness, so a real player feels a wider gap.
