# Radiance — Domain of the Sun: Building Designs

Same format and depth as the Verdance building specs (Everwood Spire, Bloomforge, Groveheart Nexus, Cycle Sanctuary, Thornwall Gatehouse, Sylvan Waystone). Follows the unified RTS building list for Radiance: Sunforge Citadel, Solar Crucible, Sunstone Vault, Blazing Bastion, Heliarch Spire, Dawnwatch Beacon, Radiant Shrine, Solar Relay, Pyrestorm Battery, Emberline Gate, plus one addition (Phoenix Roost) needed to house the flying units.

## Radiance style rules (apply to every building)

- **Style:** Celestial solar architecture. Sun-temple geometry: radial plans, sunburst crowns, tall arched windows that throw visible beams. Buildings are lit from within as if they contain a captured sunrise.
- **Materials:** Warm white marble veined with gold, burnished gold and brass fittings, translucent sunstone panels that glow amber, polished copper roofing that catches highlights.
- **Color palette (kept from the current art theme):** gold `#FFCC44`, sunstone amber `#FFA726`, dawn white `#FFF4D6`, deep bronze shadow `#3F3014`, sky-blue accent `#6EC6FF` for celestial elements. In the game engine the Radiance tint is `#FFCC44` on a `#3F3014` body.
- **Shape language:** Upward-reaching, symmetrical, radial. Rays, rings, discs, spires. Nothing organic; every curve is a sun-arc.
- **Domain iconography:** The Radiant Sigil, a twelve-rayed sun disc, embossed in gold over every entrance; a smaller sigil crowns each spire. Tier upgrades add rays to the sigil (8 → 12 → 16).
- **Construction FX (all buildings):** A column of sunlight falls on the site; the structure rises inside it as if being cast from light, marble solidifying from the top down. On completion the beam collapses into the sigil with a chime.
- **Ambient sound bed:** low choral drone, faint crackle of warm fire, occasional bell tone.
- **Tier progression rule:** T1 stone and brass, modest glow. T2 gold inlay and visible internal light. T3 a sunburst crown of floating gold rays and constant beams of light from windows.

## 1. Sunforge Citadel — Main Base / Headquarters

**Gameplay role.** Command centre. Trains Sunbearers and heroes, receives Sunstone, spreads sunlight (the Radiance ground layer) in a wide radius, unlocks each tier. Largest structure; must read as the sun of the base.

**Core aesthetic.** A stepped marble ziggurat crowned by a great gold sun-disc. Beams of light fall from the disc to the courtyard below. Gold inlaid rays run down the terraces from the disc to the ground like rivers of light. Sigil: the full Radiant Sigil forms the disc itself.

**Scale and layout.** 3×3 footprint, tallest building. Four radial stairways meet at a central sunwell where Sunstone is delivered. Hero altars at the four cardinal points.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1 — Sunforge Citadel:* stone ziggurat, brass disc, soft amber glow at the sunwell. Sunlight radius 5 tiles.
- *Tier 2 — Solar Crown:* the disc turns to gold and lifts free of the roof, hovering; gold rays inlaid on every terrace begin to glow. Sunlight radius 8.
- *Tier 3 — Zenith:* a second, larger ring of floating gold rays orbits the disc; permanent beams of light pour from the windows; the courtyard floor becomes glowing sunstone. Sunlight radius 12. Phoenix Feathers are channelled here.

**Iconic features and FX.** Floating sun-disc rotates slowly; beams sweep the courtyard; motes of gold light rise when a hero is summoned; a heartbeat pulse of light when a tier upgrade completes.

## 2. Solar Crucible — Unit & Weapon Production

**Gameplay role.** Primary military production. Trains Dawn Legionaries and Solar Archers; at Tier 2 the Crucible expands to forge Sunforged Knights and Pyrestorm Engines. Researches Blessed Steel and Sunforged Plate. +8 supply.

**Core aesthetic.** A round forge-hall with a domed roof split by a wide skylight; a shaft of focused sunlight falls through it onto a central anvil of sunstone. Weapons are tempered in light, not fire. Gold-banded marble walls, copper dome.

**Scale and layout.** 2×2 footprint, medium. Single wide portal on the front, arched drill-yard alcoves around the dome.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1:* brass dome, narrow skylight, single anvil beam.
- *Tier 2:* dome plated in gold, two additional side forges with their own beams, stables annexe for Sunforged Knights.
- *Tier 3:* dome opens into a sunburst crown; the anvil beam becomes a permanent pillar of light visible across the map.

**Iconic features and FX.** Anvil beam flares white when a unit completes; sparks of gold light instead of embers; hammer strikes sound like bells.

## 3. Sunstone Vault — Resource Storage / Refinery

**Gameplay role.** Built on a Sunstone node. Drop-off point for Sunbearers, refines a trickle of Sunstone on its own, adds +4 supply. High-value economic target.

**Core aesthetic.** A squat octagonal treasury of marble with a domed brass lid; gold-barred windows glow amber from the Sunstone stored inside. Refined stone is visible as glowing ingots stacked behind grilles.

**Scale and layout.** 2×2 footprint on the node. Ramps on two sides for Sunbearer traffic; a central skylight lets sunlight "charge" the stone.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1:* plain octagon, brass lid, faint glow.
- *Tier 2:* gold-inlaid rays on the lid, brighter glow, a second storage ring.
- *Tier 3:* lid lifts on gold pillars; the stored Sunstone shines like a small sun and throws light outward.

**Iconic features and FX.** Deposited Sunstone flashes as it enters the vault; a soft heat shimmer above the lid; coins-on-marble sound on drop-off.

## 4. Radiant Shrine — Unit Buff & Healing

**Gameplay role.** Trains Sun Priests, heals nearby units (3 hp/s), generates Dawnlight (0.4/s), spreads sunlight strongly. Required for Tier 2.

**Core aesthetic.** An open-air colonnade of white marble around a basin of liquid light. Twelve slender gold pillars, no roof, so sunlight falls directly on the basin. Serene, quiet, the most "temple" of the set.

**Scale and layout.** 2×2 footprint. Open on all sides; wounded units gather at the basin.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1:* eight pillars, shallow basin, gentle glow.
- *Tier 2:* twelve gold pillars, deeper basin, healing motes rise in a steady stream.
- *Tier 3:* a floating ring of light hovers over the basin; healing pulses ripple outward as expanding gold rings on the ground.

**Iconic features and FX.** Healing pulse every few seconds; motes drift toward wounded units; choral swell when a hero is healed here.

## 5. Blazing Bastion — Defensive Tower

**Gameplay role.** Main defensive tower. Fires beams of concentrated sunlight at ground and air; deals +25% damage when standing in sunlight. Spreads a small sunlight radius so it powers itself.

**Core aesthetic.** A tall marble tower with a gold lens-array at its crown, like a lighthouse. The lens focuses sunlight into a firing beam. Bronze buttresses, arrow-slit windows glowing amber.

**Scale and layout.** 1×1 footprint, tall. Fits into Emberline Gate walls.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1:* single lens, brass housing.
- *Tier 2:* triple lens array in gold, sigil on the parapet.
- *Tier 3:* the lens crown floats free and rotates; a continuous faint beam sweeps the perimeter even when idle.

**Iconic features and FX.** Firing: lens flares white, a straight beam with a bright impact bloom. Idle: slow lens rotation, heat shimmer. Sound: rising hum then a crack of light.

## 6. Emberline Gate — Defensive Gate / Wall

**Gameplay role.** Wall segment. Blocks ground movement. Cheap, quick to raise, chains into curtain walls with Blazing Bastions at the corners.

**Core aesthetic.** Sun-tempered marble blocks with a glowing amber seam ("ember line") running along the top of the wall where the segments meet. Gold-capped merlons. Gates in the line are tall brass doors with the sigil split across both leaves.

**Scale and layout.** 1×1 segments; contiguous segments visually merge into a single wall with a continuous ember line.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1:* plain marble, faint ember seam.
- *Tier 2:* gold-capped merlons, brighter seam.
- *Tier 3:* the ember line becomes a running river of light that pulses toward any point under attack.

**Iconic features and FX.** Gate doors swing inward in a burst of light for allies; when damaged the ember seam flares; sound of stone on stone, a bright chime on opening.

## 7. Dawnwatch Beacon — Detection & Vision

**Gameplay role.** Long-sight sensor tower. Reveals hidden units, spreads sunlight, fragile. Placed on high ground and forward positions.

**Core aesthetic.** A slender gold needle on a marble tripod base, topped by a small captured sun in a crystal cage. Light spills down the needle in slow waves.

**Scale and layout.** 1×1 footprint, very tall and thin.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1:* brass needle, small amber sun.
- *Tier 2:* gold needle, sun brightens, a ring of light circles the base.
- *Tier 3:* the caged sun floats above the needle and sweeps a visible searchlight beam across the fog.

**Iconic features and FX.** Searchlight sweep; a bright flash when it reveals a hidden unit; high crystalline hum.

## 8. Heliarch Spire — Technology / Upgrade Center

**Gameplay role.** Research centre (Daybreak, Radiant Wards, Phoenix Fire, Eternal Dawn) and Dawnlight trickle (0.5/s). Required for Tier 3. At Tier 3 the Spire forges Solar Titans and, with a Phoenix Feather, the Avatar of Dawn. Channels one Phoenix Feather every 150s.

**Core aesthetic.** A tall observatory tower of white marble with a gold armillary sphere at its summit: nested rotating rings tracking the sun. Windows throw thin beams. The most scholarly, intricate building of the set.

**Scale and layout.** 2×2 footprint, second tallest after the Citadel. Library rings around the base; the forge for Titans opens at the rear at Tier 3.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1 (built at Tier 2):* single armillary ring, brass.
- *Tier 2:* three nested gold rings, each rotating on its own axis; research completion sends a pulse up the tower.
- *Tier 3:* rings separate and float; a sun-forge opens at the base; a Phoenix Feather is visible burning in the sphere while it charges.

**Iconic features and FX.** Rotating armillary; a beam from the sphere to the sky when research finishes; the Feather flares when ready. Sound: ticking clockwork under the choral drone.

## 9. Solar Relay — Map-wide Power Projection

**Gameplay role.** Global ability structure. Solar Surge: for 15s every Radiance unit gains +20% damage and towers +50%. Spreads a wide sunlight radius. Fragile; enemies will target it.

**Core aesthetic.** A wide, low marble dish (a solar mirror) tilted toward the sky, ringed by twelve gold focusing pylons. When idle, it slowly gathers light; when fired, the dish blazes and a pillar of light rises to the sky.

**Scale and layout.** 2×2 footprint, low profile with a tall pillar effect only when active.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1:* brass dish, six pylons.
- *Tier 2:* gold dish, twelve pylons, standing beam while charged.
- *Tier 3:* dish floats and tracks the sun; Surge fires a visible wave of light across the whole map.

**Iconic features and FX.** Charge indicator is the dish brightness; Surge: dish flash, sky pillar, gold ring expanding outward over the terrain. Sound: rising tone to a bell strike.

## 10. Pyrestorm Battery — Artillery / Siege Structure

**Gameplay role.** Long-range static artillery (10 tiles). Slow, heavy solar shells with splash; ground targets only. Anchors a siege line or a fortified expansion.

**Core aesthetic.** A heavy marble bastion housing a great bronze "sun cannon": a tilted barrel of nested gold rings that gathers light and hurls a molten sun-shell. Bronze buttresses, heat-blackened marble at the muzzle.

**Scale and layout.** 2×2 footprint, squat and heavy.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1:* single barrel, brass rings.
- *Tier 2:* gold rings, cooling vanes that glow between shots.
- *Tier 3:* twin barrels; shells leave trails of gold fire.

**Iconic features and FX.** Charging glow along the barrel rings; the shell is a small blazing sun with a fire trail; impact leaves a brief burning sigil on the ground. Sound: deep resonant boom.

## 11. Phoenix Roost — Flying Unit Production (addition)

**Gameplay role.** Not in the unified list; required to house the Phoenix Heralds and Radiant Angels. Trains Phoenix Heralds; at Tier 3 Radiant Angels. +6 supply. If a canon building is later chosen for fliers, this becomes that building.

**Core aesthetic.** A ring of tall gold perches around a marble brazier of eternal solar fire; phoenixes roost in the flames. Open to the sky; feathers of light drift down constantly.

**Scale and layout.** 2×2 footprint, open plan, perches rise above the walls.

**Tier progression.**
- *Tier 1:* four perches, small brazier.
- *Tier 2:* eight gold perches, tall flame, the brazier becomes a landing platform.
- *Tier 3:* a floating ring of fire above the roost where Radiant Angels descend.

**Iconic features and FX.** Falling feathers of light; the brazier flares when a flier completes; wing-beat whoosh and a phoenix cry on training.

## In-engine representation (2D, kept consistent with the current build)

Until modelled art exists, every Radiance structure is drawn in the current programmer-art style: a `#3F3014` bronze body, `#FFCC44` gold outline, a gold triangle glyph for the Citadel, a disc for towers, a filled inner square for halls, and a three-letter abbreviation. Sunlight is the pale gold ground overlay. The Godot port uses the same colors and glyphs so the theme is unchanged across engines.
