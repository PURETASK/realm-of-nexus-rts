# Tempest Faction – Domain of Chaos, Storms, and Speed (Version 2)

> Source: Realm of Nexus RTS Design Bible drafting session, Aug 2025 (second Tempest sheet, produced after the Volume 1 PDF review).

## Faction Summary (Playstyle & Strategic Identity)

Tempest is the domain of chaos, storms, and unrelenting speed, shaped by Auranth – the Architect of Storms – into an ever-changing maelstrom that uses unpredictability as its greatest weapon. Its playstyle revolves around rapid strikes, extreme mobility, and dynamic tactics that keep opponents off-balance. Tempest armies are built to hit hard and fade away before the enemy can react, often attacking from the skies or from multiple directions at once. They harness the fury of storms – sudden lightning, howling winds, torrential rain – and turn these into weapons of war.

Tempest forces emphasize skyborne mobility and relentless initiative. A typical engagement begins with a barrage of thunderbolts from afar, followed by Stormriders and dragoons flanking the enemy's edges, while Windborne Scouts capture distant objectives or cut off reinforcements. Tempest doesn't hold territory in slow, attritional ways – they move like a storm front: overwhelm a point with superior force, then flow on before the enemy's response arrives. This is mastery through chaos. In lore, Tempest's elite form sacred pacts with storm dragons (the Stormbind Accord), so dragon-riding warriors soar alongside their forces.

Tempest's chaos is controlled chaos – the faction excels when dictating the flow of battle. If something robs Tempest of its momentum, their advantage crumbles. Tempest units are lighter and rely on not getting hit; if forced into a prolonged slugfest, they are vulnerable. "From the storm, we rise" captures the creed: weaponize unpredictability and speed, but avoid the calm after the storm where weaknesses are laid bare.

## Main Base Upgrade Path

- **Tier 1 – Stormpeak Outpost:** A mobile or semi-floating base platform tethered to the ground (a chunk of floating island anchored by chains, with an Aether-powered generator at its core). Trains workers (Aeromancers / wind Engineers) and initial infantry. Defining feature: the Outpost can relocate short distances by packing up and floating (limited early, perhaps to leyline range). Modest defense: a small Tempest Coil field that zaps nearby enemies with weak lightning.
- **Tier 2 – Skyborne Bastion:** The outpost fully lifts off its ground tethers and hovers. Unlocks mid-tier units, flying units, and economy upgrades. Active defense **Stormfront Shield** – a swirling wind barrier that deflects incoming projectiles for a short duration. Passive **Aerodynamic Aura** slightly boosts movement speed of nearby allies. Unlocks **Sky Gates** – a teleportation network linking two points on the map for near-instant unit transport.
- **Tier 3 – Stormspire Nexus:** A grand island lifted into the sky with a towering spire crackling with perpetual lightning. Grants all late-game units and ultimate technologies (Cyclonic Constructs, dragons). Built-in ability **Eye of the Storm**: creates a massive storm in a wide radius, dramatically slowing and damaging enemy units. May also allow global casts (supercharged Chain Lightning anywhere, a ~30-second global storm). Ultimate mobility: the entire Nexus may teleport to a secured location on the map once in a while.

## Resource System

- **Primary – Aetherium (Stormstone) Crystals:** Sparkling blue-white crystals containing the essence of sky and storm, mined from lightning-struck locations and high mountaintops. Base currency for units and buildings, and fuel for aether-tech. Unique mechanic: nearly exhausted Aetherium nodes can be partially regenerated by casting a localized mini-storm on them. Encourages nomadic, high-risk harvesting.
- **Secondary – Zephyrite Gas (Elemental Air):** A volatile gas captured with condensers or siphons from vents in windy canyons or thick storm clouds (analogous to Vespene Gas). Required for advanced aether-tech, magical research, and higher-tier units. Tempest can deploy floating siphon rigs or kite-like devices. When weather intensifies (naturally or by Tempest's doing), more Zephyrite can be harvested – Tempest is rewarded for causing chaos.
- **Legendary Catalyst – Eye of the Storm (Astral Core):** A glowing astral crystal said to be the condensed heart of an ancient perpetual storm. Earned by capturing and holding all neutral wind shrines, slaying an elemental guardian, or completing a grand ritual. Only one per match. One-time use: trigger a cataclysmic world-wide storm (Tempest units massively buffed, enemies severely debuffed), summon a Storm Avatar (colossal storm dragon or elemental titan) for a limited time, or slot into the Stormspire Nexus to make the base impervious or relocate it without harm.

## Heroes

### Stormblade Commander (Military Hero)

Frontline champion in storm-forged armor, riding a tamed Stormdrake, wielding a spear or paired curved blades that crackle with every swing.

- **Thunderbolt Charge:** Dashes in a straight line through multiple enemies with electrifying force; heavy damage, stun or knockback.
- **Whirling Tempest:** Spinning attack that whips up a mini-tornado, slashing nearby foes and hurling them back.
- **Zephyr Aura (Passive):** Nearby Tempest units gain movement and attack speed.

Peerless in skirmishes and duels; not built for protracted engagements.

### Aethermancer Engineer (Economic/Support Hero)

Part inventor, part strategist, part field mechanic, carrying a portable aether-tech workshop or exo-suit.

- **Turbocharge:** Temporarily doubles a friendly building's output or work rate.
- **Field Repair Drones:** Hovering drones rapidly repair damaged mechanical units for a short duration.
- **Aero Shield:** Swirling wind barrier over an area or unit that significantly reduces incoming ranged damage.
- Passive: increased resource gathering for nearby workers and faction-wide build speed. May construct utility structures like a **Sky Beacon** (extends Sky Gate range, high-altitude balloon vision).

### Tempest Sorcerer / Stormcaller Sage (Magical/Utility Hero)

Veteran mage of atmospheric magic; robes ever billowing, crowned with crackling energy.

- **Chain Lightning:** Strikes a target and leaps to several additional enemies.
- **Fog Shroud:** Thick fog bank over an area, greatly reducing visibility.
- **Eye of the Storm (spell):** Calm zone at a target location encircled by a raging wind wall; allies inside are safe, enemies entering or firing through struggle.
- **Gale Transport:** Turns a squad of allies into a whirling gust for rapid repositioning to a visible point (short-range teleport).
- **Maelstrom (Ultimate):** A colossal moving tornado that sucks up and hurls units; does not discriminate friend from foe.
- Can dispel or manipulate weather effects (cleansing winds against poison, redirecting enemy smoke).

## Buildings & Production Structures

- **Aerodrome (Barracks):** Hybrid barracks and flight academy. Trains Windborne Scouts (fast light infantry with gliders), Stormriders (shock sky cavalry on storm drakes), and Skyguard Sentries (anti-air ground infantry). Upgrades: Cyclone Training, Aerodynamic Armor. Enables **Airlift** – garrisoned units ferried between any two Aerodromes or Sky Docks.
- **Cyclone Spire (Mage Guild):** Trains Stormweavers (elemental sorcerers) and Skycallers (weather/buff support). Upgrades: extended Chain Lightning targets, Storm Amplification, Astral Feedback (enemy casts near Tempest units trigger backlash lightning). Unlocks global **Localized Storm** cast anywhere with vision; multiple Spires stack or allow simultaneous casts.
- **Aetherforge (Workshop):** Trains Skybreaker Constructs (flying gyrocopter-like machines with thunderbolt weapons), Cyclonic Constructs (ground golems generating tornadoes), and Lightning Cannons (electrical siege artillery). Upgrades: Overcharge Capacitors, Lightweight Alloy. Produces **Storm Barrier** generators – paired devices projecting a wall of turbulent wind that slows and damages enemies passing through.
- **Sky Dock (Support & Economy):** Floating pier of balloons and airships. Functions as a market (convert Aetherium ↔ Zephyrite), runs aerial trade routes via Trade Zeppelins / Cloud Barges for extra income, receives paid reinforcement airdrops, and raises population cap (Tempest's housing equivalent). Losing a Sky Dock reduces population and trade income.

## Defensive Structures

- **Lightning Tower:** Slender spire with a focusing storm crystal. Strikes with a thunderbolt that chains to nearby enemies; periodic heavy bursts rather than continuous fire; targets air. Upgrades: Overcharge Mode, "ionized" debuff (+20% damage from subsequent hits). Gains rate of fire and range during rain or storms.
- **Wind Guard Turbine:** Giant rotating turbine emitting hurricane-force winds in a cone or circle. Enemies barely advance; ranged attacks lose accuracy and range. Minimal direct damage. Modes: narrow jet or wide dispersal. Upgrades: razor wind blades, auto-targeting.
- **Cloud Citadel (Shield Generator):** Floating platform with spires forming a translucent cloud dome. Everything underneath takes reduced damage from outside attacks. Possibly a one-time shield pulse (near invulnerability for a second or two). May be slowly movable to accompany armies.
- **Sky Mines:** Deployable hovering drone-mines that dart toward approaching enemies and detonate in an electric flak burst. One-use, flexibly placed, provide slight vision. Especially effective against fliers and light units.

## Progression Flow (T1–T3 Development)

- **Tier 1 (Early Game):** Speed and initiative. Windborne Scouts and the Stormblade Commander establish map control, scout, and harass worker lines. Relocate the Stormpeak Outpost toward new resource nodes as old ones deplete. Avoid fair fights against heavy Tier 1 infantry; be everywhere the enemy is not. Fog Shroud covers retreats; a Wind Guard Turbine thwarts rushes. Goal: extensive vision, delayed enemy expansions, multiple secured nodes, and no catastrophic army losses.
- **Tier 2 (Mid Game):** Combined arms come online with Stormriders and Skybreaker Constructs. Multi-pronged assaults via Sky Gates force the opponent to split defenses. Sky Dock trade routes supplement income but are vulnerable to enemy air interception. The Aethermancer Engineer boosts production and fortifies outposts with Storm Barriers and Sky Mines. Lightning Towers and Wind Guard Turbines make direct assaults costly. Bait slow armies (Sanctuary, Abyss hordes), punish overextensions, snipe support units. Never take a fair pitched battle against heavier factions like Radiance.
- **Tier 3 (Late Game):** Unleash full fury. Ultimate units (Voidstorm Dragons, Astral Dreadnought) add raw power to finesse. Use the Eye of the Storm catalyst at the decisive moment to force the enemy to fight inside a realm-wide tempest. The Stormspire Nexus can teleport itself and part of the army behind enemy lines or initiate a base race. Max-level heroes layer storms across the battlefield. If forced onto the defensive, fall back behind Storm Barriers, Cloud Citadel domes, mines, and supercharged towers. Tempest must ensure the climax happens on their terms; a straight fight against an equal late-game army favors the opponent.

## Strengths & Weaknesses

- **Strengths:** Overwhelming speed and dynamic tactics; masters of initiative and surprise. Relocate across the map in the blink of an eye via fliers and teleportation. Excel in hit-and-run, choosing when and where to fight. Storm powers grant unparalleled battlefield control (lightning, wind scatter, fog). Adaptive economy via regenerating mines and Sky Dock trading. Fighting Tempest is like fighting a storm: hard to see coming, explosive, gone before you hit back.
- **Weaknesses:** Lacks staying power – mobility and damage over armor; suffers in prolonged head-on fights or sieges. A disciplined enemy who protects flanks and economy and weathers the initial onslaught blunts Tempest raids. Micro-heavy and unforgiving; mis-timed storms get fragile units caught. May fall behind in a long macro game if aggression fails. Disproportionately crippled by anti-magic or anti-machinery effects (EMP, silence). Crumbles quickly if forced into a defensive posture without setup. Tempest must win by brilliance and speed.
