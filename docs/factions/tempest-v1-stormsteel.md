# Tempest Faction – Domain of Storms and Skies (Version 1)

> Source: Realm of Nexus RTS Design Bible drafting session, Aug 10, 2025 (first Tempest sheet, produced alongside Abyss).

## Faction Summary (Playstyle & Strategic Identity)

The Tempest faction exemplifies mobility, adaptability, and aerial supremacy. Its playstyle is that of a hit-and-run tactician and weather master – Tempest forces strike swiftly from the skies and withdraw before a counterattack can land. Strategically, Tempest is about controlled chaos: the faction harnesses the chaotic power of storms to disrupt enemies and empower its own troops, embodying the philosophy "master the chaos, and you master yourself." Tempest's identity is defined by air dominance and storm-based battlefield control. Most of its units can fly or have special movement capabilities, allowing a Tempest commander to outmaneuver opponents, bypass terrain, and raid vulnerable targets at will. Instead of brute force, Tempest relies on precision and timing – using abilities that summon thunderstorms, gusting winds, and lightning strikes to confuse and cripple foes at critical moments. Economically, the faction uses a tribute-based resource flow: rather than heavy on-map harvesting, Tempest establishes conduits and forges that draw energy and materials from their domain (and vassals) as tribute. This results in a steady stream of resources that scales with map control and infrastructure. Tempest is unpredictable and reactive – it can tech-switch quickly using elemental catalysts. Its armies might consist of floating constructs, storm mages, and sky knights that excel in coordinated strikes. The faction's weakness lies in its relative fragility and reliance on keeping momentum; in a protracted head-on engagement, their units can be glass cannons. Tempest's strategic identity is the tempest itself: ever-shifting, overwhelming if unchecked, and gone before you can grasp it.

## Main Base Upgrade Path (Tier 1 → Tier 3)

- **Tier 1 – Stormspire Outpost:** A fortified outpost built around a shard of the Maelstrom Crown. *Unlock Condition:* Starting structure. *Benefits:* Trains workers and basic units; passively generates a small trickle of Tribute (primary resource). Has a short-range anti-ground zap (lightning rod) and a limited "Stormsense" ability (slightly increased line of sight during storms). Limited build radius until upgraded.
- **Tier 2 – Skybreaker Citadel:** A grand floating bastion empowered by council magics. *Unlock Condition:* Requires Stormsteel (primary) and some Aether Crystals (secondary), plus a Tier 1 tech structure (e.g. Stormcall Tower). *Benefits:* Unlocks advanced unit production (airships, storm elementals), allows a second hero, boosts Tribute income rate and capacity. Gains "Tempest Barrier," a defensive aura that reduces damage from ranged attacks for nearby units/buildings. Greater build radius; enables small global weather abilities via research.
- **Tier 3 – Maelstrom Nexus:** An airborne fortress fully fused with the raging storm. *Unlock Condition:* Requires Tier 2, a substantial stockpile of Stormsteel + Aether Crystals, and at least one Tempest Pearl (legendary catalyst). *Benefits:* Unlocks all top-tier units and technologies; permits the third hero. Grants global aura "Eye of the Storm" – during any active storm ability, Tempest units take reduced damage and move faster. Enables ultimate abilities (one-time mega-storm or a sky titan). The Nexus can call down thunderbolts on attackers and may relocate a short distance (slowly floating).

## Resource System

- **Primary – Stormsteel (Tribute):** A unique Tempestian alloy forged from stormglass ore and lightning, serving as both material and currency. *Gathering:* Aetherforges and Trade Outposts built on resource nodes or in base automatically refine stormglass into Stormsteel and send it as "tribute." The primary base also provides a slow trickle. Enemies can raid forges or pillage outposts. *Usage:* Backbone resource for most units and buildings, plus physical unit upgrades.
- **Secondary – Aether Crystals:** Glowing shards of condensed storm energy. *Gathering:* Harvested from Aether Wells or storm nodes via Stormcall Arrays built by Windcasters (workers). Also from residual aether pickups after storms, and periodic shipments at Tier 2+. *Usage:* Elite units, spellcaster abilities, large storm abilities, elemental imbuement upgrades. Relatively plentiful but fluctuates with weather and map control.
- **Legendary Catalyst – Tempest Pearls:** Rare orbs of crystallized storm essence. *Gathering:* Extraordinary means only – retrieved from the Tempestfall Cascades, earned by weathering a massive storm event or slaying an elemental mega-creature, or slowly generated by a fully upgraded Stormcaller's Tower at Tier 3. *Usage:* "Eye of Auranth" super ability (map-wide violent tempest), awakening the Sky Titan, or amplifying hero ultimates. Other domains (Radiance, Sanctuary) also value them for artifacts.

## Heroes

### Stormlord Rykan (Military Hero)

A seasoned sky-knight and tactician, a frontline melee hero riding a flying mount (gryphon or small drake).

- **Thunderstrike:** Lightning-infused strike (melee or thrown lightning javelin). High burst plus splash; briefly stuns (especially mechanical units).
- **Wind Rally:** War cry boosts allied movement speed significantly and attack speed slightly in a medium radius. Ideal for hit-and-run initiation.
- **Skyfury Aura (Passive):** Allied flying units near him gain armor and damage. His attacks chain lightning to up to 2 nearby enemies.
- **Eye of the Storm (Ultimate):** A localized storm cloud centers on him and moves with him. Nearby allies take significantly reduced damage and deal bonus lightning damage; enemies inside suffer reduced movement and random lightning strikes.

### Skybreaker Lyrian (Economic/Support Hero)

A member of the Skybreaker Council renowned for logistics and engineering. Not a strong combatant; carries a staff of winds.

- **Trade Winds:** Temporarily increases Stormsteel and Aether income rates and speeds production queues. Must be channeled briefly; moderate cooldown.
- **Zephyr Ward:** Swirling wind barrier at a location that reduces incoming projectile damage by a large percentage, reveals invisible units, and slightly heals structures.
- **Aerial Logistik (Passive):** Windcasters carry +1 resource per trip; Aetherforges generate bonus Stormsteel; transports move faster with more HP. Garrisoned structures gain armor and regen.
- **Call of the Armada (Ultimate):** After a brief delay, a contingent of Tempest airships and shocktroopers arrives at the map edge under your control, at no resource cost. Composition scales with game time.

### Stormcaller Alyssia (Magical/Utility Hero)

Direct descendant of the first Tempest Stormcaller; a fragile but powerful storm mage.

- **Chain Lightning:** Bounces between up to 6–7 targets with reduced damage per jump; stuns mechanical/summoned units for 1 second.
- **Cloudburst:** Localized storm cloud slows enemy movement and attack speed (~30%) and deals light damage; allies are healed slightly and slowed less.
- **Eye of Clarity:** Reveals a large map section (or the whole map briefly), dispelling fog of war and invisibility. Long cooldown.
- **Hurricane Maelstrom (Ultimate):** A massive slow-moving hurricane. Ground units nearly immobilized, projectiles wildly inaccurate, flying units forced down or tossed, random lightning strikes, reduced vision. Does not discriminate friend from foe (Tempest units slightly more resilient). Alyssia sits at the calm eye.

## Buildings & Production Structures

### Tier 1

- **Gale Barracks:** Trains Stormblades (lightning-charged swordsmen) and Wind Archers (long-range crossbowmen). Houses infantry upgrades.
- **Aerie (Skyworks Level 1):** Trains Skywings (flying scout) and Cloud Skiffs (light flying shooter). Upgrades to Skyworks at Tier 2.
- **Aetherforge:** Hybrid economy/tech structure that refines stormglass into Stormsteel and Aether. Each one increases passive Stormsteel income.
- **Stormcall Tower (Tier 1):** Weak lightning defense plus prerequisite for weather research ("Forecast," "Static Shield").

### Tier 2

- **Skyworks:** Trains Thunderhawks (medium fighters with stormrifles), Tempest Riders (elite flying cavalry vs ground), and Zephyrships (light dirigible transports with storm bombs).
- **Storm Tower (Stormcall Tower upgrade):** Chain-lightning defense and weather controller: "Static Charge" (targeted strike) and "Microburst" (knockback gust). Detects cloaked fliers.
- **Tempest Forge:** Trains Storm Golems (heavy melee constructs) and Lightning Cannons/Ballistae (anti-structure siege). Possibly Skybreaker Constructs.
- **Stratosanct:** Floating tech hub. Researches Elemental Catalyst tech, tribute efficiency, second hero unlock. Slowly generates Aether Crystals.

### Tier 3

- **Stormseer Conclave:** Ultimate mage guild. Trains Tempest Weavers (battlefield-wide weather manipulators) and Sky Oracles (vision/support). Researches top-tier spells.
- **Celestial Bastion:** Capital ship production: Sky Leviathan (flying artillery platform) and Storm Titans (giant elemental in mechanical chassis). Requires Tempest Pearls or a fully teched economy.
- **Maelstrom Altar:** Channels legendary powers (Tempest Pearl world-storm), unlocks the final hero, provides global regen for air units.
- Infrastructure note: at Tier 3 the main base and possibly Storm Towers can relocate slowly.

## Defensive Structures

- **Thunderhead Tower:** Lightning bolts prioritizing flying targets; chains to a second target. Multiple towers focusing one target amplify damage. Requires Aether to charge (low Aether slows fire rate). Upgrades: more chains, EMP slow.
- **Cyclone Totem:** No direct damage. Projectiles entering its zone are heavily reduced or miss; enemies moving toward it are slowed, retreating enemies sped up. Vulnerable to melee.
- **Tempest Barrier (Wall):** Semi-solidified wind/aether walls. Harden against siege hits, repel melee attackers, cannot be flown over while active. Require power from base or Storm Tower; collapse if the power source dies.
- **Storm Beacon (Sensor Outpost):** Extends vision, detects stealth, can create a local fog bank or heavy rain infrequently. Defenseless; best on high ground.

## Progression Flow

- **Early Game (Tier 1):** Focus on mobility and scouting. Get an Aerie and Skywings out quickly to survey and harass. Build an Aetherforge on the nearest node. Secure map control points and place a Storm Beacon. Against rushes, rely on Thunderhead Towers and Cyclone Totems, Alyssia's Cloudburst, or Rykan's Wind Rally to kite. Constant hit-and-run and feints.
- **Mid Game (Tier 2):** Aerial supremacy kicks in via Skyworks. Dominate the skies, target enemy scouts and siege. Tribute economy booms with multiple Aetherforges and Aether Wells. Bases become tough with Storm Towers and Tempest Barriers. Multi-pronged attacks: drakes hit one expansion while a Zephyrship drops Stormblades elsewhere. Use Eye of Clarity to stay ahead. "Choose your battlefield."
- **Late Game (Tier 3):** Shift from guerrilla to overwhelming force at critical points. Combine Hurricane Maelstrom, Rykan's Eye of the Storm charge, and Lyrian's Call of the Armada for a perfect storm. Use Sky Leviathans for siege and Tempest Pearls from the Maelstrom Altar to break stalemates. Never fight on enemy terms; disengage faster than any other faction.

## Strengths & Weaknesses

### Strengths

- **Unrivaled Mobility:** Fast, mostly flying units grant map control and the choice of when/where to engage.
- **Aerial Supremacy:** Dominates the skies; forces enemies to invest in anti-air.
- **Weather Control & Crowd Control:** Storm abilities slow, blind, stun, or halt armies, letting a smaller force win.
- **Strong Economy Scaling:** Autonomous Aetherforges and Lyrian's buffs mean Tempest often outraces other factions in income by late game.
- **Adaptability and Versatility:** Wide range of roles and damage types; can tech-switch quickly via elemental catalysts.

### Weaknesses

- **Fragile Units:** Lower health/armor at equivalent tiers; glass-cannon tendencies demand skilled micro.
- **Micro & Multitasking Demands:** High APM playstyle; underperforms if not handled actively.
- **Dependence on Map Control:** Tribute economy crumbles when pushed off key nodes.
- **Weather Abilities Can Be Countered:** Weather-clearing magic, waiting out durations, or friendly-fire misuse.
- **Siege and Sustained Damage Limitations:** Struggles against heavily fortified positions and very sturdy, well-healed targets.
