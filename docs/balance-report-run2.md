# Balance Report

150 AI-versus-AI matches, every faction pairing (including mirrors), difficulties easy / normal / hard, two map seeds each. Player 0 is a normal-parameter AI in the human seat; player 1 is the opponent AI with the difficulty's income multiplier (the other difficulty parameters, attack timing and build delay, apply to both AIs). Matches are capped at 1200 game seconds. Generated by `tools/balance_report.py` from the Godot build.

## Findings

- Strongest faction in AI hands: Sanctuary (66% win rate). Weakest: Verdance (25%). A spread wider than about 20 points means the rosters, not the AI, are unbalanced.
- 24 matches hit the 1200 s cap without a winner; those pairings stall into a stand-off.
- Tier 3 was reached in 12 of 300 seats. Ultimates and Tier 3 units barely feature in AI games, so Tier 3 balance is untested.
